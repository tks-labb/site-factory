# Email and Text Templates

Replace everything in `[brackets]`. Keep messages short. The demo link does the selling.

---

## 1. First text (after a call, or cold)

> Hi [Owner], it's [Your name]. I build websites for local businesses in [Area]. I made a free demo site for [Business]. Take a look: [demo link]. Happy to make changes. Want it live on your own web address?

## 2. Cold email with demo

**Subject:** I built a website for [Business]

> Hi [Owner],
>
> I build websites for local businesses in [Area]. I noticed [Business] has [great reviews on Google] but [no website / a site that's hard to use on a phone], so I built you one:
>
> **[demo link]**
>
> It has your services, hours and reviews, a one-tap call button, and a contact form that sends new leads straight to your email.
>
> If you like it, I can put it on your own web address this week. If not, no problem at all.
>
> [Your name]
> [Your business] · [phone]

## 3. Follow-up #1 (day 5)

**Subject:** Re: I built a website for [Business]

> Hi [Owner], just checking you saw the site I made for [Business]: [demo link]
>
> Anything you'd want changed? I can update it the same day.
>
> [Your name]

## 4. Follow-up #2 (day 10)

**Subject:** Quick question about [Business]

> Hi [Owner],
>
> Most people find a local [plumber/salon/etc.] on their phone and call whoever looks most trustworthy. Your reviews already do half the work. The site does the rest.
>
> The demo is still up: [demo link]. Launch is [$X], then [$Y]/month for hosting, updates and the lead form. Want me to set it up?
>
> [Your name]

## 5. Final email (day 20)

**Subject:** Taking down your demo site Friday

> Hi [Owner],
>
> I'll take the demo for [Business] offline this Friday to make room for other clients. If you'd like to keep it, just reply "yes" and I'll get it live on your own address.
>
> Thanks either way,
> [Your name]

## 6. Proposal (after they say yes)

**Subject:** Your new website: next steps

> Hi [Owner],
>
> Great talking with you. Here's what's included:
>
> - Your website on **[domain]**, mobile-friendly and secure (https)
> - Contact form that emails every lead to **[their email]**
> - [Booking link / Google review button / other extras]
> - Hosting, security and small updates every month
>
> **Price:** [$X] to launch, then [$Y]/month. Cancel the monthly any time with 30 days' notice.
>
> **Next steps:**
> 1. Sign the agreement: [link]
> 2. Pay the launch fee: [payment link]
> 3. Fill in this short form (logo, photos, domain details): [intake link]
>
> Once those are in, your site is live within [3–5] business days.
>
> [Your name]

## 7. Domain access request

**Subject:** One thing I need to put your site live

> Hi [Owner],
>
> To connect the site to **[domain]**, I need access to the account where the domain was bought ([GoDaddy/Wix/etc.]). Two easy options:
>
> 1. Add me as a delegate/manager on the account using **[your email]**, or
> 2. Hop on a 10-minute screen share and I'll walk you through it.
>
> I only change the website settings. Your email will keep working exactly as it does now.
>
> [Your name]

## 8. Launch announcement

**Subject:** Your new website is live 🎉

> Hi [Owner],
>
> **[domain]** is live! Have a look on your phone too.
>
> - New messages from the contact form go to **[their email]**. Check your spam folder for the first one.
> - Need a change? Just reply to this email or text me.
>
> One favor: if you're happy, would you leave me a quick Google review? [your review link]. And if you know another business owner who needs a site, I'd love an intro.
>
> Thanks for trusting me with this,
> [Your name]

## 9. Review request (for the client to send their customers)

> Thanks for choosing [Business]! If you were happy with our work, a quick Google review helps a lot: [their review link]
