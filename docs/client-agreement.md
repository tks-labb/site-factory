# Website Services Agreement (Template)

> **Before you use this:** this is a plain-language starting template, not legal advice. Laws differ by state. Have a local attorney review it once, then reuse it. Fill in everything in `[brackets]` and delete this box.

---

**This agreement is between:**

- **Provider:** [Your business name], [address], [email], [phone] ("Provider")
- **Client:** [Client business name], [address], represented by [owner name], [email], [phone] ("Client")

**Effective date:** [date]

## 1. What the Provider will deliver

1. A website for the Client's business, as shown in the approved demo at [demo link], with these pages/sections: [home, services, reviews, contact, …].
2. A contact form that emails submissions to [client email].
3. Connection of the website to the Client's domain, **[domain]**, with a security certificate (https).
4. [Optional extras: booking link, Google Business Profile updates, AI chat agent, …]

**Not included unless added in writing:** online stores, logo design, photography, copywriting beyond the approved demo, paid advertising, and ongoing SEO campaigns.

## 2. Timeline

The Provider will launch the website within **[5] business days** after receiving (a) the signed agreement, (b) the launch fee, and (c) the Client's materials and domain access. Delays in receiving these move the timeline accordingly.

## 3. Fees and payment

| Item | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Launch fee | $[X] | Due before work starts. Non-refundable once the site is launched. |
| Monthly care plan | $[Y]/month | Billed on the [1st] of each month, starting the month after launch. |

The monthly care plan includes: hosting, security certificate renewal, keeping the contact form working, and up to **[30 minutes / 2 small changes]** of content updates per month (text, prices, hours, photos). Larger changes are billed at $[rate]/hour, quoted before work starts.

Late payments: if a monthly payment is more than **[15] days** late, the Provider may pause the website after written notice until payment is received.

## 4. Revisions

The launch fee includes **[2] rounds** of revisions to the approved demo before launch. Additional revisions are billed at $[rate]/hour.

## 5. Client responsibilities

The Client will:

1. Provide accurate business information, and only provide photos, logos, reviews and text they have the right to use.
2. Provide domain access (or make DNS changes as instructed).
3. Review the site before launch and approve it in writing (email is fine).
4. Keep their domain registration paid and renewed, unless the Provider registered the domain on the Client's behalf (see section 6).

## 6. Ownership

1. **Domain:** the domain belongs to the Client. If the Provider buys it for the Client, it is registered in the Client's name or transferred to the Client on request, and its yearly cost ($[amount]) is [included in the care plan / billed to the Client].
2. **Content:** the Client owns their business content (text, logo, photos, reviews).
3. **Website files:** once the launch fee is paid, the Client receives a license to use the website design for their business. [Choose one: **(a)** The Client owns the final website files and may request a copy at any time. **(b)** If the Client cancels within the first [12] months, they may buy the website files for $[amount].]
4. The Provider may reuse its general templates, code and know-how for other clients, and may show the Client's site in its portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.

## 7. Cancellation

1. Either party may cancel the monthly care plan with **30 days' written notice** (email is fine).
2. On cancellation, the Provider will, at the Client's request, (a) provide the website files as described in section 6, and (b) help point the domain to a new host. After the notice period, the Provider stops hosting the site.
3. Fees already paid are not refunded, except where required by law.

## 8. Form submissions and data

Contact form submissions are stored by the hosting provider ([Netlify]) and emailed to the Client. The Provider will only access them to troubleshoot or when the Client asks, and will not sell or share them. The Client is responsible for how they use customer information they receive.

## 9. Limits

1. The Provider will use reasonable care to keep the site online but does not guarantee 100% uptime, specific search rankings, or a specific number of leads.
2. The Provider is not responsible for outages or changes caused by third parties (hosting provider, domain registrar, email providers).
3. Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the Client paid in the **[3] months** before the claim.

## 10. General

This is the full agreement between the parties and replaces earlier discussions. Changes must be in writing (email is fine). This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [state].
