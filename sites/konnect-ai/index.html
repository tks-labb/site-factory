Questions

Before you book a call.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You explain how the work gets done today. We handle the design, the building and the connections, and we train your team at launch.

What can you actually automate?

If a person does it the same way more than a few times a week, we can likely automate most of it. That covers answering leads, handling calls, reading documents, updating records, sending follow-ups and building reports.

Will it work with the software we already use?

Almost always. We connect to your CRM, email, phones, calendars, spreadsheets and industry tools. If a tool has no standard connection, we build one.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the system. Small automations can be live quickly, and larger multi-step systems take longer. You get a timeline in the written plan before any work starts.

What happens after launch?

We monitor the system, fix anything that comes up, and keep improving it. As your business changes, the system changes with it.
