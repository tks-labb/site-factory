    Questions

    Before you book a call.

    Do I need to be technical?

    No. You explain how the work gets done today. We handle the design, building and connections, and train your team at launch.

    What can you actually automate?

    If a person does it the same way more than a few times a week, we can likely automate most of it: answering leads, handling calls, reading documents, updating records, sending follow-ups and building reports.

    Will it work with the software we already use?

    Almost always. We connect to your CRM, email, phones, calendars, spreadsheets and industry tools. If a tool has no standard connection, we build one.

    Do you build websites too?

    Yes. We design and build mobile-first websites for local businesses, with tap-to-call, lead forms that email you, online booking and Google-ready setup. We also handle hosting and updates.

    How long does a build take?

    It depends on the system. Websites and small automations can be live quickly; larger multi-step systems take longer. You get a timeline in the written plan before any work starts.

    Is the chat on this site real AI?

    Yes. Click "Ask our AI" in the corner and try it. It's the same kind of assistant we build for clients, trained on information about Konnect AI.

    What happens after launch?

    We monitor the system, fix anything that comes up, and keep improving it. As your business changes, the system changes with it.
