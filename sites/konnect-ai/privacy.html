Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Konnect AI ("we") collects through konnectai.org and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, email, phone number and message when you use our contact or estimate forms.
  • Chat messages: the messages you type into our AI assistant, so it can reply.
  • Booking details: if you book a call, the scheduling tool collects the details you enter.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider may log standard information such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability.
  • Activity on the site: the Meta Pixel records page views, form submissions and booking-link clicks (see below).

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and provide our services.
  • To send you the information you asked for, such as an automation estimate.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the website: Netlify (hosting and form handling), Google (calendar booking), and Anthropic (the AI model behind our chat assistant). They process information on our behalf to provide these services.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you opt in to receive text messages from Konnect AI, we collect your mobile phone number and your messaging consent. We use this information only to send the messages you agreed to receive. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers (such as our messaging platform) only as needed to deliver messages to you. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Advertising and measurement

We use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, to measure visits and actions on this site (such as page views, form submissions and booking-link clicks) and to show and measure our ads on Facebook and Instagram. Meta may use cookies and receive information such as your IP address and browser details. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings and limit tracking in your browser.

Chat assistant

The AI assistant is automated and may make mistakes. Please don't share sensitive personal information, such as financial or health details, in the chat.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you've sent us by emailing tristan@getkonnectai.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: tristan@getkonnectai.com or (313) 474-9885.
