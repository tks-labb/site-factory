Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Ammori Group ("we", "us") collects through ammorigroup.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, email, phone number, what you're interested in, your message, and any tour date or time you request when you use our inquiry form.
  • Space alert sign-ups: your email, the property you asked about, and what you're looking for, if you tell us.
  • Chat messages: the messages you type into our AI assistant, so it can reply.
  • Saved in your browser only: your light or dark mode choice and your space shortlist are stored on your own device. We don't receive them unless you send us an inquiry that lists them.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider may log standard information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To answer your inquiry, arrange tours, and tell you about space that fits what you asked for.
  • To email you when space opens up, if you signed up for alerts.
  • To discuss buying your property or business, if you contacted us about selling.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the website. They process information on our behalf only to provide their service:

  • Netlify: hosting and form handling. Form submissions are stored by Netlify and emailed to us.
  • Anthropic: the AI model behind our chat assistant. Chat messages are sent to Anthropic to generate replies.
  • OpenStreetMap and Cloudflare (cdnjs): map images and the map software. Your browser contacts them when a map loads, which shares your IP address with them.
  • Google Fonts: the typefaces on the site. Your browser contacts Google when pages load.
  • Google Maps: only if you click "Get directions" or "Open Street View", which opens Google Maps under Google's own privacy policy.

AI chat assistant

Our chat assistant is automated and may make mistakes. It does not make offers, quotes or commitments on our behalf; lease terms and pricing are agreed only in writing. Please don't share sensitive personal information, such as financial or health details, in the chat.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiries and alert sign-ups as long as needed to respond and to manage our leasing and acquisitions, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you've sent us, or to stop sending space alerts, by emailing info@ammorigroup.com.

Children

This website is for businesses and adults looking for commercial space. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
