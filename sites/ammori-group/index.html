About

Owners, not middlemen.

Ammori Group is a Bloomfield Hills real estate company that owns and operates commercial property across Metro Detroit and Ann Arbor. We don't manage buildings for other owners. Every property in our portfolio is our own, which means decisions about leasing, improvements and service are made by the people who own the building.

For tenants, that means direct conversations, flexible layouts and a landlord who is invested in the property for the long term.
