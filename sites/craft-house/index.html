Questions

Good to know

What areas do you serve?

Oakland, Wayne and Macomb counties in Michigan. If you're close by and not sure, call or text us.

Can we have more than one bar?

Tell us which bars you're interested in on the form. You can pick as many as you like, and we'll follow up with options for your event.

How do I get pricing?

Send a request with your date, location, guest count and the bars you like, and we'll send you the details for your event.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekends. Send your date and we'll let you know if it's available.
