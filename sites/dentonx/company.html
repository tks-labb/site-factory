Enabling connected financial ecosystems.

DentonX builds data infrastructure and automation for the lenders, platforms and investors working beyond traditional banking.

Mission & vision
Our mission is to enable connected financial ecosystems through data infrastructure and automation.

Our vision is a shared technology layer for lending and investing, where every partner works from the same data and every decision leaves a clear record.

Our story

DentonX was founded in California in 2025 to bring modern infrastructure to non-bank lending, a fast-growing part of finance that still runs on spreadsheets, email and one-off integrations.

We're a technology company, not a lender. Licensed, independent partners make the lending decisions. We build the systems that connect their data, automate their workflows and keep the records.
