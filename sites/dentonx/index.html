How it works

From raw data to a recorded decision.

Every loan file moves through the same five stages, so partners get consistent data and a complete record.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Bank statements, credit, property and payment data arrive from partners in real time.

  2. 02

    Normalize

    Data is cleaned and standardized into one consistent format.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Business rules and approval workflows run automatically for each partner.

  4. 04

    Record

    Every action is written to a secure, tamper-evident record.

  5. 05

    Report

    Dashboards and audit trails keep lenders, servicers and investors aligned.

Our model

We build the software. Licensed partners lend.

DentonX is a technology provider. Regulated lending activity is carried out by licensed, independent partners on the platform.

What DentonX does

  • Builds data infrastructure for lending partners
  • Automates workflows, decisions and compliance steps
  • Provides analytics, audit trails and reporting
  • Connects lenders, servicers and investors on one layer

What DentonX doesn't do

  • Originate loans or extend credit
  • Underwrite financial products
  • Broker securities or manage capital
  • Provide consumer financial services
