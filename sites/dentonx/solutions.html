Markets

Where the platform focuses.

Two lending markets where borrowers often fall outside traditional bank criteria.

Non-QM mortgage

Loans outside the Qualified Mortgage standard, often for self-employed borrowers and real estate investors. About 10% of U.S. mortgage dollar volume in 2025.

  • Bank statement loans
  • Investor / DSCR loans
  • Asset-based loans
  • Self-employed borrowers

SME lending

Credit for small and mid-sized businesses. The share of small-business applicants seeking financing at online lenders rose from 17% to 29% between the 2020 and 2025 Federal Reserve surveys.

  • Term loans
  • Lines of credit
  • Equipment financing
  • Online and alternative lenders

Sources: Polygon Research (non-QM, 2025 HMDA data); Federal Reserve Small Business Credit Survey.
